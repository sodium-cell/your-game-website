THE WORLD OF REAWAKENING

LORE

Humanity and the Awakened once shared the same world.

But when people began to fear those who possessed supernatural abilities, the fragile relationship between the two sides collapsed.

The Awakened were hunted. Families were torn apart. And the truth behind the conflict slowly disappeared beneath generations of hatred.

◇

HUMANS

Ordinary people who fear the power of the Awakened. Some seek safety, while others believe the Awakened must be eliminated.

✦

THE AWAKENED

People born with extraordinary abilities. Some desire peace, while others have lost faith in humanity completely.
