ABOUT US

ReAwakening: Savior of Memories is an independent game created from passion, imagination, and a love for storytelling.

We are a small group of young creators building our own world from scratch.

From the characters and lore to the music, coding, and visual design, every part of the game is made with our own ideas and creativity.

This is our story. And this is where it begins.

LORE

A world divided by fear, power, and forgotten memories.

HUMANS

Humans live in fear of the Awakened. Their powers are considered dangerous, leading to conflict between the two sides.

Many believe that the only way to protect humanity is to eliminate the Awakened.

AWAKENED

The Awakened are people who possess mysterious supernatural abilities.

Some seek peace. Others believe humanity can never accept them. Their existence becomes one of the biggest mysteries of the world.

ASTER

Aster wakes up without memories of his past.

With fragments of a forgotten life slowly returning, he begins searching for the truth behind his identity.

THE TRUTH

Somewhere between Humans and the Awakened lies a truth that has been forgotten.

To uncover it, Aster must confront the past he no longer remembers.
